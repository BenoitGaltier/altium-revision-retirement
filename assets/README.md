# Fluidra logo

Source: [Fluidra corporate website logo](https://www.fluidra.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/fluidra-logo.svg), referenced by the [official press room](https://www.fluidra.com/press-room/).

Retrieved 3 November 2026. `fluidra-logo.svg` is the original white wordmark. `fluidra-logo.png` rasterizes its original paths without changing their shape or colour. The two extension thumbnails place the same wordmark on a blue background; the header embeds the transparent PNG into the extension assembly.

The Fluidra name and logo remain the property of Fluidra. They are included for the requested internal branding of this private extension, without implying Altium certification.
